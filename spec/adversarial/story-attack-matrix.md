## Cross-Suite Attack Rules

- CH-EASE handoffs must reject unknown target apps and incompatible package kinds.
- Publisher apps must reject package data that would make token products masquerade as contract products.
- Shadownet tests must fail closed on mainnet fallbacks or relay endpoints.
- WTF.ME cannot report a page live until the public URL actually serves the expected bundle.
- WTFOS pinning cannot report complete until the provider CID and recovery state are known.
- Colander cannot offer write actions unless the contract entrypoint exists; chain remains final authority.
