⬤ Macaroni Studio

1 · Network

Run a full Shadownet rehearsal before mainnet. Free test tez: shadownet faucet.

2 · Drop details

Delayed: collectors mint blanks showing your cover image. You can reveal at any time from step 6 — and once the oldest unrevealed mint is older than the window, anyone can trigger the reveal from the mint page, so collectors are never stuck. The window can be changed later but never above 30 days.

3 · Tokens

Upload a CSV with columns id, name, description (extra columns become traits). Then upload your artwork files named by token id (1.png, 2.jpg, …). Download CSV template.

4 · Media storage (IPFS)

Your art and metadata are pinned to IPFS.

5 · Sale stages

At least one stage is required. Stages must start in chronological order. Allowlist CSV format: one tz1…address,capacity per line.

Resume an existing drop

Already deployed? Paste your contract address or import a saved file to continue sync, design, and export. If this browser lost your draft, re-import the same CSV + artwork to change stages — on-chain token metadata stays loaded once sync completed.

6 · Deploy & sync on-chain

Deploys your own FA2 contract (it appears as a standalone collection on objkt), then loads token metadata, stages and allowlists in batched transactions. You pay only network fees — there is no platform fee.
