What this app is (and isn't)

Macaroni is a build tool for creator-owned drops. Nobody mints in the studio and nothing is listed here. Collectors mint on the page you publish to wtfOS or export for your own host. The drop.html page in this folder is just the live preview of your design.

FA2 / TZIP-12 · TZIP-16 · TZIP-21 compatible — collections appear on objkt.com automatically
