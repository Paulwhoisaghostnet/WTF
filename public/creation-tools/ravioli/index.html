1 · Storage / pinning

Wrapper metadata, pack manifests, and generated output are pinned by the creator or collector. Downloaded builds can use Pinata or a local Kubo node.

2 · Router + blind controller

First collection: the reusable router/controller pair has an estimated one-time Tezos origination and storage cost of about 11.3 tez, plus normal variable fees. Reuse that same pair for many packs.

“Add a pack to a router I administer” is the normal repeat-drop path. A new collection deploys its blind controller first, permanently binds that controller to the router, then records both addresses for Colander.

Ownership relationship (optional)

3 · Wrapped product

Blind packs are Limited Editions: the complete finite wrapper supply is sold before reveal. Ravioli checks every Pasta-native child window before any pin or transaction.

Blind ordering is enforced as primary sale end < reveal deadline < delivery/refund cutoff. Any enclosed LE also forces a finite Ravioli wrapper supply and finite sale ending strictly before that child’s earliest public mint expiry; reveal must occur no later than the expiry. The open/refund cutoff may be later because child capacity is already reserved, so delivery does not reopen public issuance.

4 · Atomic recipe

Every recipe is fully escrowed or capacity-reserved before wrappers can mint. If any child transfer or mint fails, Tezos reverts the entire opening and the wrapper remains owned.

Blind means commit/reveal and normal UI concealment—not chain privacy. Funding and reservation operations remain visible on Tezos.

Automatic allocation / generative adapter setup
Advanced per-edition recipe matrix

Optional JSON array with exactly one action array per edition. Use this for a blind funded pool whose allocations differ. Empty uses the visible recipe for every edition.

5 · Fund, lock, and issue

The wallet will sign origination/configuration, escrow or capacity reservations, finalization, wrapper minting, and optional sale operations. Rehearse on Shadownet first.

          Transfer an unopened wrapper
          
Transfer moves the wrapper and its exact top LIFO blind claim together. Unrevealed wrappers freeze at the reveal deadline; revealed wrappers freeze at the open/refund cutoff.

          

            
            
          

          

        

        

          Expired claim refunds
          
After the applicable cutoff, anyone may credit an unopened claim’s refund to its current holder. Funds never go to the caller. The credited holder then withdraws separately, so a rejecting destination cannot block pack closure and a failed withdrawal leaves the credit intact.

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
          

          
Use an implicit tz account for normal withdrawal. Advanced contract testing may enter a KT1; if that contract rejects tez, the whole withdrawal reverts and the holder’s credit remains available.

          
After the reveal deadline, anyone may close an unrevealed pack only when every purchased claim is refunded and controller escrow is zero. Holder withdrawal credits remain available after closure.
