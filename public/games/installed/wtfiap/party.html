Game rules

Ancient keeps hero shops, shared copies, combining, leveling, traits, and battles. Modern enables all optional systems. Room rules lock when created.

Cherub, battle plans, shared draft, and supply encounters use items. Zone rewards apply only on a Zone Defense board.

Tournament prizes: 1st 50% · 2nd 35% · 3rd 15%. Only human entrants pay; AI fill plays the remaining seats. Tournament XTZ entries use Tezos Shadownet; WTF entries use Tezos Mainnet. Duel XTZ entries show a separate network choice. A wager entry must be a multiple of 0.01 in the room currency. Room creation takes no payment.
